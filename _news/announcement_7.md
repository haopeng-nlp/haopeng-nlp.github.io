Continuously consolidating useful experiences into LLM-written memory can eventually make agents worse than using no memory; preserving raw episodes and gating consolidation is more reliable—see our new preprint [Useful Memories Become Faulty When Continuously Updated by LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12978).
